# Output Contract

## Required Shape

Data Flow, Ownership, Compatibility, Migration/Backfill Plan, Idempotency, Failure Handling, Verification Queries, Rollback.

## Always Include

- Summary
- Evidence Reviewed
- Risk Level
- Confidence
- Recommendation or Change
- Verification
- Open Questions or Blockers

## Quality Bar

- Specific file paths or runtime signals beat generic advice.
- Explain why a risk matters, not just that it exists.
- Make the next step executable.
